03 · Role Clarity

Know what success looks like

Clear expectations from the beginning make it easier to learn, perform and ask for help when needed.

Your role at a glance

Your manager should help you understand your responsibilities, daily priorities, field activities, expected outcomes and the support available to you.


  • Understand your designation and reporting structure.
  • Know your key day-to-day responsibilities.
  • Understand field activities and operating processes.
  • Know where to go when you face an operational blocker.

Your support network

Reporting Manager
Role guidance, coaching, targets and performance conversations.


Buddy / Peer
Practical help with settling into the team and daily processes.


HR
People-related support, onboarding and employee queries.
