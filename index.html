02 · Journey & Targets

First 90 days roadmap with target clarity

A phased growth trajectory with clear focus areas, progressive target expectations, and dedicated coaching at every milestone.

💡
Immediate Action Item for Day 1–2:

Connect with Sumit from Training tomorrow to walk through your personalized 90-day learning journey, branch schedule, and channel target roadmap.

Priority Action
Days 0–7

Settle In & Learn

Complete joining formalities, connect with Sumit from Training, meet your Reporting Manager & Buddy, and achieve 100% portal & product certification.

Target Clarity: Zero sales pressure. 100% focus on product mastery, branch orientation & joint shadowing.
Days 8–30

Joint Field Work

Shadow your manager/buddy on live customer visits, understand distributor and partner channels, and practice customer need-discovery.

Target Clarity: Complete 10–15 joint customer calls, activate first 2–3 agent/partner leads, close first milestone policy.
Days 31–60

Build Momentum

Conduct independent customer meetings, expand agent/channel relationships, track weekly pipeline health, and review conversion metrics.

Target Clarity: Achieve 60%–75% of standard monthly run-rate with weekly manager coaching cadence.
Days 61–90

Full Scale & Mastery

Drive autonomous sales execution, master multi-product solutions (Activ Health, Activ One), build referral funnels, and celebrate 90-day progress.

Target Clarity: 100% monthly target achievement, self-sustained pipeline, 90-day milestone appraisal with HR.
